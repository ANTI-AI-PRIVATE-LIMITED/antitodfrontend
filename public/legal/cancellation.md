# Cancellation Policy

* You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing page in your account, or by emailing support@antiai.ltd.
* Cancelling stops future renewals. You will not be charged again.
* Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it ends.
* When the plan ends, log collection and exports stop. Your data is kept for 30 days so you can renew and carry on. After that it is deleted.
* Free trial: you can cancel any time during the 14 day trial and you will not be charged.
