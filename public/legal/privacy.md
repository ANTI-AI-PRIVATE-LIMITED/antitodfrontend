# Privacy Policy

**Who we are.** ANTI AI Private Limited is the data fiduciary (controller) for the account data of people who sign up for ANTITODE. For the honeypot logs you send, you decide the purpose and we process them for you as your processor.

## 1. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, work email, organisation, role, password (stored as a salted hash), login history, one time codes | Create and secure your account |
| Billing data | Plan, status, payment dates, Razorpay customer and subscription ids. **We do not store card numbers**, Razorpay does | Charge and support you |
| Honeypot data you send | Event logs, source IP addresses, ports, commands, uploaded files and similar attacker activity from your sensors | Provide the dashboard, scoring and exports |
| Enrichment data we add | Country, city, network (ASN), Tor and cloud flags, reputation scores | Make the data useful |
| Integration settings | Connector URLs and tokens, stored encrypted | Send alerts and exports where you ask |
| Technical data | IP address, browser, requests, error logs, rate limit counters | Run, secure and fix the Service |
| Support messages | Emails and chat you send us | Help you |

## 2. How we use it

To provide and secure the Service, bill you, send service emails (confirmation, one time codes, password reset, billing and security notices), give support, improve reliability, and meet legal duties. We do not sell your data and we do not use your honeypot data to train AI models.
Marketing emails are only sent with your consent and you can opt out in every message.

|---|---|---|

## 4. Where data is stored

Primarily in [AWS Mumbai region, India]. Some providers above process data in other countries. Where data leaves India we rely on the protections available under applicable law [and contractual terms with those providers].

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Honeypot events | Basic plan 30 days, Pro plan 90 days, then deleted automatically |
| Account data | While your account is active, then deleted or anonymised 30 days after the plan ends |
| Billing records | 8 years where tax law requires |
| Logs and backups | Up to 90 days |
| Unverified sign ups | Deleted after [a few days] if the email is never confirmed |

## 6. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, hashed passwords, role based access, per customer data separation, rate limiting, audit logs and a secrets vault. See our security page for more. Report vulnerabilities to dev@antiai.ltd.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to nominate someone to act for you, as provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law. Email support@antiai.ltd. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating.
For honeypot data about third parties (attackers), requests should go to the customer who deployed the sensor.
