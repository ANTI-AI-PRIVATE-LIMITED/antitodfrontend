# Refund Policy

* Subscriptions are billed in advance, monthly, in Indian rupees (INR).
* **First payment:** if you are not happy within 7 days of your first paid payment, email support@antiai.ltd and we will refund that payment in full.
* **After that:** payments are not refunded, but you can cancel to avoid the next renewal.
* **Mistakes:** if we charge you in error or charge you twice, we refund the extra amount in full.
* **Plan changes:** if you upgrade part way through a month, we charge only the difference for the rest of the period.
* Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
